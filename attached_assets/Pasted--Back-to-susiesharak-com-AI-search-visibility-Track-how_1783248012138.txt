Top fixes

Move core content out of sr-only hidden container

The entire visible text body — including the H1, credentials paragraph, and FAQ dl/dt/dd block — is wrapped in a sr-only div styled with clip, overflow:hidden, and 1px dimensions, making it invisible to sighted users and likely discounted by AI crawlers that cross-reference rendered output. Move this content into the normal document flow using semantic HTML so it is part of the actual rendered page AI engines index.

Add sourced, verifiable citations to the credentials claims

The page claims 'Ranked in the top 5% of real estate agents by Hour Detroit Magazine (2024)' and '$100M+ in career sales / 500+ homes sold' but provides no hyperlink or external reference. Add an anchor tag linking directly to the Hour Detroit listing or a verifiable third-party source, and cite the MLS or brokerage record for the transaction volume so AI engines can treat these as citable facts rather than unverified self-claims.

Expand FAQ answers with specific, data-backed local market statistics

FAQ answers such as 'homes sell in 10 to 14 days' and '$350,000 to $600,000+' are stated without a source year, data provider, or MLS citation. Append each answer with a source reference (e.g., 'per REALCOMP MLS Q1 2025 data') and expand the home-sale-prep answer into a numbered checklist so AI engines can surface it as a structured snippet.

Add a LocalBusiness or Person schema with verified review source links

The aggregateRating block shows 4.9 stars across 87 reviews but does not include a 'url' property pointing to the review platform (Google, Zillow, Realtor.com) where those reviews live. Add a 'sameAs' or review source URL to the aggregateRating and link each Review entity to a public profile, otherwise AI engines cannot verify the rating and are unlikely to surface it in rich results.

Implement speakable content in rendered HTML, not only JSON-LD

The speakable SpeakableSpecification targets '.speakable-answer' CSS selectors, but those elements are hidden inside the sr-only container and will not match rendered DOM that AI voice and overview systems parse. Place at least two .speakable-answer paragraphs in the visible body with a direct, question-answering opening sentence (e.g., 'Susie Sharak is a top-5% Realtor in Royal Oak, MI, with 500+ homes sold across Oakland and Macomb County.') so the selector resolves to indexable content.
