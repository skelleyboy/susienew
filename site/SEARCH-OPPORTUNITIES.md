Source: the Search Console export supplied on November 9, 2026.
The export filter says “Last 16 months”; the daily chart contains 193 dates,
April 29 through November 7, 2026.

- Desktop: 32 clicks, 290 impressions, 11.03% CTR.

Page-level totals and property totals are different aggregations and must not
be added together. The query export exposes only seven queries, mostly names,
and four clicks for “susie sharak.” It does not reveal the queries responsible
for most clicks. Do not label all traffic branded or invent keyword volumes.
The export contains no form submissions or conversion-rate data. Device CTR
does not establish which device converts better.

## Work prioritized from that evidence

1. Make the selling offer specific: address/city, pricing, repairs and timing.
2. Give Sterling Heights visitors both buying and selling paths, a useful
   local checklist and a city-specific inquiry prompt.
3. Recover the indexed Grosse Pointe legacy URL with a permanent redirect to
   a real, canonical community guide, not to the homepage.
4. Keep the homepage identifiable as Susie Sharak's local Realtor site.
5. Reduce form friction, support mobile autofill, and include submission page,
   first landing page, referrer host and optional campaign labels with leads.
6. Keep content server-rendered, native forms functional without JavaScript,
   clear canonicals, a current sitemap and genuine 404 responses.

## What still needs live validation

These are site changes, not proof of ranking gains or delivered inquiries.
Publish the static build on the existing Netlify site, verify all four form
names and email notifications there with an approved test submission, and
request indexing of the priority pages in Search Console.

Compare the next 28 days with a comparable prior period after publication:
page impressions/clicks, non-brand query visibility where available, and real
inquiries by submitted source page. With this traffic level, do not promise
daily leads or infer statistical significance from a few submissions.
