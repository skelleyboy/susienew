---
name: External real estate profile checks
description: Automated-access denials on authentic profile sites must not be mistaken for missing profiles.
---

Do not replace Susie's verified profile links with generic directory pages solely because an automated link checker reports an error.

**Why:** During the November 9, 2026 visibility review, the site's internal links loaded normally while Zillow, Homes.com and FastExpert denied automated access, Realtor.com rate-limited it, and LinkedIn used its bot-block response. These were not evidence that the actual profiles were missing.

**How to apply:** Distinguish internal broken pages from external anti-bot responses. Verify an external destination independently before changing it, and clearly separate published-site checks from fixes that are still only in the workspace.
