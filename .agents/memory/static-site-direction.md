The user requested that the whole site become raw HTML, feel real rather than AI-generated, address today's market, and focus on local searches and getting leads.

**Why:** The user wants a credible, searchable real estate business site, not a generic AI-feeling presentation.

**How to apply:** Preserve complete readable HTML and native lead forms without requiring JavaScript. Make useful local content visible to people and crawlers alike. Use sourced, dated market figures and real identity assets rather than unsupported rankings or generated testimonials.

The user supplied three high-quality real estate website references and asked to get as close as possible to that quality, using styles we can execute well and consistent branding and styling.

**Why:** The visual standard is an established professional real estate business, not a generic generated website.

**How to apply:** Carry one recognizable brand through every page, not just the homepage. Use the references for visual quality without copying another agent's identity or fabricating listings and achievements.

The user explicitly corrected that palette and spacing changes were not close enough to the references and that Susie needs a proper logo. They then asked to put her photo at the top, use less introductory and small text, make everything easy to read, design mobile-first, and use nice spacing.

**Why:** The user wants a complete personal brand and an immediately human, readable phone experience, not surface restyling.

**How to apply:** Keep Susie's real photo prominent near the top, concise introductory copy, legible type, comfortable mobile gutters and touch targets, and a proper consistent logo rather than plain initials in a generic badge.

The user specifically requested a 12-column desktop grid.

**Why:** The user wants the alignment and composition discipline seen in the reference sites.

**How to apply:** Align desktop page sections to shared 12-column tracks and common content edges; adapt deliberately to fewer columns on phones.

The user requested removing Golden Realty text from the header and making the main paths lead to contact forms too.

**Why:** The user wants the personal brand in the header and more direct lead capture throughout the site.

**How to apply:** Do not reintroduce brokerage text into the header. Keep appropriate brokerage identification elsewhere. Give marketing pages a native inquiry form and main conversion actions a direct form destination without breaking navigation or phone links.
